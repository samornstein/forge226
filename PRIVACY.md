# Forge226 personal WHOOP integration privacy policy

Connecting WHOOP is optional. The personal integration is intended to read sleep and recovery records, including sleep duration, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and recovery score, for training and recovery reviews.

Imported records are stored in the owner’s local Forge226 athlete data store. Sharing exports, publishing dashboards or supplying records to a coaching assistant makes the selected information available to that recipient or service. Forge226 does not sell WHOOP data or use it for advertising.

Revoke access in WHOOP to stop future retrieval. Previously saved data remains until the owner deletes the local records and any exported or shared copies. For privacy or deletion questions, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub. Keep health records and credentials out of public issues.
